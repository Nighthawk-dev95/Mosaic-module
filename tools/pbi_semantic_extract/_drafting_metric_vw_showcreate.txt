  GRAIN. One row per exam-reading, inherited unchanged from edw_prod.enterprise_analytics.tbl_exam_inventory. Every count here is a count of exam-readings. The view carries ONE join (see THE DYNAMICS CONSENT JOIN below) and it is verified fan-out-free, so the grain is unchanged by it.
  MANDATORY FILTER, BAKED IN AND NOT OPTIONAL. The source restricts to Study_Status_Code = 'Final'. All three draftability levels key on final_datetime, so unfinalized and billing-only exams always read FALSE and would silently deflate every rate. The filter is in the source rather than in each measure so a consumer cannot forget it. Any count from this view is a count of FINALIZED exams.
  THE DYNAMICS CONSENT JOIN, ADDED 2026-09-04, AND ITS COVERAGE LIMIT. The source LEFT JOINs edw_prod.enterprise_analytics.dynamics_rad_drafting_consent_vw on CAST(npi AS STRING) = reading_provider_npi, to expose radiologist enrollment state as a dimension. NO FAN-OUT, VERIFIED TWICE: the consent view is one row per NPI (5,486 rows, 5,486 distinct NPIs, zero NULL NPIs, live 2026-09-04) and the August 2026 finalized row count is identical before and after the join (3,922,923 both ways). Nothing already in this view moved. THE COVERAGE LIMIT IS THE POINT TO CARRY, AND THIS SECTION IS ITS ONE OWNING LOCATION: the consent view matches roughly 51% of the radiologists who finalized an exam in the trailing 90 days, being 3,549 of 6,933 distinct reading NPIs over final_datetime from 2026-06-06 to 2026-09-04, measured 2026-09-04. It moves. The same measure read 3,549 of 6,928 earlier on 2026-09-04, identical numerator with five more readers in the denominator after that day's rebuild, which is exactly why the figure is stated once here and referenced, not restated, everywhere else in this view. Every enrollment-state measure and dimension here is therefore blind on roughly half the reading fleet, and an unmatched radiologist is NOT an unconsented one. A missing record is never an accusation.
  ENROLLMENT DECOMPOSITION USES CARLTON PRICKETT'S CATEGORY LABELS ON PURPOSE (Yaro Kobzar, 2026-09-04). The measures named "Rad not enabled - ...", "Practice not enabled for model" and the Workflow synonym "Drafting Workflow Opportunity" carry the Clinical Transformation Team's own wording so that RP has one shared vocabulary for the drafting gap instead of two. THE LABELS ARE HIS; THE SQL BEHIND THEM IS OURS, and the two have not been reconciled field by field (see THE ATTRIBUTION RECONCILIATION below). Matching a label is not evidence of matching a definition.
  *** THE CONSENT-TO-CATEGORY MAPPING IS UNCONFIRMED AND IS THE WEAKEST LINK IN THIS VIEW. *** "Ops declined" is mapped to mosaic_drafting_consent = 'No Drafting' and "Rad declined" to 'Declined'. The Unity Catalog comment on that column glosses 'No Drafting' only as "opted out", which does not say WHO opted out, and RP Brain fields/mosaic-drafting-consent.yaml (status DRAFT, v0.1.0, 2026-09-03) states the opposite of an answer: "'No Drafting' AND 'Declined' ARE BOTH REFUSAL-SHAPED AND THEIR DIFFERENCE IS NOT ESTABLISHED... Do not merge them, and do not assert which one means what, until the deployment team confirms." That entry carries an OPEN consultation to Aura asking exactly this question. This view asserts the mapping because a shared vocabulary needs one, and it flags the assertion here and in both measure comments rather than hiding it. It is the single most likely thing in this view to be wrong, and the fix is a sentence from the Clinical Transformation deployment team, not more SQL.
  *** THE ATTRIBUTION RECONCILIATION, AND IT IS THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL THING KNOWN ABOUT THIS VIEW. *** RP's attribution of the drafting gap and the Clinical Transformation Team's differ SHARPLY even though the totals nearly agree. August 2026, this view against CTT's figures scaled to a month: rad-not-enabled 21,211 against roughly 165,000; practice-not-enabled 87,346 against roughly 35,000; workflow 339,530 against roughly 148,000; total draftable-not-drafted 448,087 against roughly 400,000, which is agreement within about 12%. READ THAT THE RIGHT WAY ROUND: the two sides agree on HOW BIG the gap is and disagree on WHOSE IT IS, by a factor of roughly 8 on rad-not-enabled and roughly 2.5 the other way on practice-not-enabled. A total that reconciles is therefore NOT evidence that the split reconciles, and the split is the half that decides which lever gets pulled and which team gets the work. Do not present the agreeing total as validation of the disagreeing components. THE FIELD-LEVEL MAPPING BEHIND CTT'S SPLIT IS AN OPEN QUESTION WITH BHARGAV NARASIMHAN and is unresolved as of 2026-09-04; until it is answered, neither split is established as the right one and this view's numbers are RP's attribution, correct against the shipped columns, not an adjudication. SCOPE NOTE, LOAD-BEARING FOR ANYONE RE-DERIVING THESE FOUR FIGURES: they are on the UNSCOPED August 2026 finalized basis (no rpce source filter, no exclusion-code filter, no Northside removal), unlike the acceptance figures in the VALIDATION BLOCK below which are stated on the RP Brain scope. The three components sum to the total exactly: 339,530 + 87,346 + 21,211 = 448,087. Note also that "draftable-not-drafted" in this comparison spans THREE not-draftable reasons, two of which carry exam_draftable_flag = FALSE, so 448,087 is a deliberately broader population than Draftable Exam Count and will not reconcile against it.

  THIS VIEW REPLACES "Mosaic Draft %" AND "Mosaic Read %" FOR THIS DOMAIN. Those two measures on tbl_exam_inventory_metric_view are NOT consumable for adoption headlines: they divide by COUNT(*), which conflates eligibility with adoption and reads 28.93% against 34.93%, a 6.0 point gap and a 21% relative understatement. Use Drafting Coverage and Drafting Adoption Rate here instead, and never publish drafted / mosaic-read finals under either of those two labels.
  THE DRAFTABLE DENOMINATOR IS MOSAIC-READ VOLUME, NOT TOTAL VOLUME. Since tbl_exam_inventory v2.3.11 (2026-09-02) exam_draftable_flag carries a platform gate on mosaic_read_flag: an exam not read on Mosaic could not have been drafted whatever its RPID, practice or reader state. Every rate on the draftable basis is therefore relative to Mosaic-read volume. Verified live on August 2026: zero exams are draftable while not Mosaic-read.
  TWO TREND BREAKS, NEITHER CROSSABLE WITHOUT A BREAK NOTE. 2026-09-02, v2.3.11 added the mosaic_read platform gate. 2026-09-04, v2.3.12 REMOVED rad_drafting_enabled_flag from the composite, moving 206,713 exams all-history from FALSE to TRUE and zero the other way. Do not trend across either date and do not adjust older figures arithmetically; re-measure.
